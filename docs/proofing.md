# Proofing

**Proofing** means checking your book carefully before you share it with readers. For print books, this means ordering a proof copy and reading it. For eBooks, this means previewing it on a Kindle device or app.

Do not skip proofing. It is your last chance to catch errors before readers see them.

## Proofing a print book

### Order a proof copy

Once your print book is live (or even during setup, depending on your marketplace), you can order a proof copy — a single copy of your book printed at cost, not for sale.

1. In KDP, go to your paperback or hardcover book.
2. Click **Order author copies** or **Order a proof**.
3. Select one copy and your shipping address.
4. Place the order. It arrives in about a week (varies by location).

Proof copies may say "Not for Resale" on the cover. That is normal.

### Read the proof

When your proof arrives, read through it carefully:

- **Cover:** Is the title readable? Is the spine straight? Are colors right? Is there a barcode?
- **Interior:** Read every page. Look for typos, awkward line breaks, and odd spacing.
- **Page numbers:** Do they appear where expected?
- **Chapter headings:** Do they start on new pages?
- **Images:** Are they clear and placed correctly?
- **Trim size:** Does the book feel like the size you chose?

Mark up the proof with a pen. Note every error, even small ones.

### Fix and re-upload

If you find errors:

1. Fix them in your manuscript or cover file.
2. Re-upload the corrected file(s) in KDP.
3. Your book goes back into review.
4. Order another proof if the changes were significant.

## Proofing an eBook

Use **Kindle Previewer** (a free app from Amazon) or send the eBook to your Kindle device/app:

- Check that the table of contents works.
- Check that text reflows correctly when you change font size.
- Check that images appear and look right.
- Check for typos and formatting problems.

## Common proofing issues

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| Typos or grammar errors | Fix in manuscript, re-upload |
| Wrong page numbers | Check manuscript headers/footers, re-upload |
| Cover image cut off | Fix cover file using KDP template, re-upload |
| Spine text off-center | Regenerate cover with correct spine width, re-upload |
| Missing table of contents | Use Heading styles in manuscript, re-upload |

## When is your book ready?

Your book is ready to share when:

- You have read the proof cover to cover.
- All errors you found are fixed and re-uploaded.
- You are happy with the cover and interior.
- You have ordered at least one proof copy (for print) and approved it.

It is normal to find small errors on the first proof. Most authors order two or three proofs before they are satisfied.
