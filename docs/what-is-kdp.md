# What Is Amazon KDP?

**Amazon KDP** stands for Kindle Direct Publishing. It is Amazon's self-publishing platform. With KDP, you can publish a paperback book, a hardcover book, or an eBook (Kindle eBook) and list it for sale on Amazon — with no upfront cost and no inventory to store.

## How KDP works

KDP is **print-on-demand**. That means Amazon only prints a copy of your book when someone orders it. You do not pay to print books in advance, you do not keep boxes of books in your garage, and you do not handle shipping. Amazon handles printing, shipping, and customer service for orders.

You earn a **royalty** — a share of each sale — minus Amazon's printing costs and their cut. We explain pricing and royalties in [Pricing & Royalties](pricing.md).

## What you can publish on KDP

- **Paperback** — a softcover print book
- **Hardcover** — a hardcover print book (available in some marketplaces)
- **eBook / Kindle eBook** — a digital book read on Kindle devices and apps

You can publish one, two, or all three formats of the same book.

## What you need to get started

- A finished manuscript (your book's text, edited and formatted)
- A book cover (front cover, and for print, a spine and back cover)
- A title and subtitle
- A book description
- Categories and keywords (so readers can find your book)
- A price
- An ISBN (we explain options in [ISBN](isbn.md))
- Bank and tax information so Amazon can pay you royalties

## What KDP is not

KDP is not a traditional publisher. Amazon does not edit your book, design your cover, or market your book for you. You are the publisher. That means you are responsible for the quality of your manuscript and cover before you upload. This guide helps you with each step.

## A quick word on rights

When you publish through KDP, you keep the rights to your book. You give Amazon a license to sell and distribute it. You can publish the same book elsewhere too, as long as you are not enrolled in KDP Select (an optional program that requires exclusivity for eBooks). You can unpublish your book at any time.
