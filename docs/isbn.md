# ISBN

An **ISBN** (International Standard Book Number) is a unique identifier for your book. It helps bookstores, libraries, and readers find and order the exact edition of your book.

## Do you need an ISBN for KDP?

**No — not required.** Amazon KDP provides a **free ISBN** for your paperback or hardcover if you do not have your own. For Kindle eBooks, Amazon uses its own identifier called an ASIN, and you do not need an ISBN at all.

## Your ISBN options

| Option | Cost | Best for | Notes |
|--------|------|----------|-------|
| Free KDP ISBN | $0 | First-time authors, simple setup | Amazon is listed as publisher. You cannot use this ISBN elsewhere. |
| Your own ISBN | Varies ($125 each in the US from Bowker) | Authors who want full control | You are listed as publisher. You can use it with other printers and bookstores. |
| Publisher/imprint ISBN | Varies | Small presses, LLCs | If you have an imprint (e.g., Asdale Dynamics), you can buy ISBNs under it. |

## Free KDP ISBN: pros and cons

**Pros:**
- Free.
- Quick — you get it during KDP setup.
- Fine for eBooks (which use ASIN anyway) and for print books sold mainly on Amazon.

**Cons:**
- Amazon is listed as the publisher of record.
- The ISBN only works through Amazon/KDP. You cannot use it to sell through other printers or bookstores.
- You cannot transfer it later.

## Your own ISBN: pros and cons

**Pros:**
- You (or your LLC/imprint) are listed as publisher.
- You own it and can use it with any printer, bookstore, or library.
- Looks more professional if you plan to publish many books.

**Cons:**
- Costs money (in the US, ISBNs are sold by Bowker; one ISBN is about $125, a block of 10 is cheaper per ISBN).
- You must buy and register it before uploading.

## Buying your own ISBN (US)

In the United States, ISBNs are sold by **Bowker** at myidentifiers.com. You can buy one ISBN or a block. After buying, you register your book's title, author, and format in Bowker's database.

**Tip:** If you plan to publish more than one book, buy a block of 10. Each format (paperback, hardcover, eBook) technically needs its own ISBN, though Amazon uses ASINs for eBooks.

## One ISBN per format

Each edition and format of your book needs its own ISBN:

- Paperback: 1 ISBN
- Hardcover: 1 ISBN (separate from paperback)
- eBook: ISBN optional (Amazon uses ASIN)

If you publish a paperback and a hardcover of the same book, they get different ISBNs.

## Where the ISBN goes

- Printed on the copyright page inside your book.
- Printed near the barcode on the back cover.
- Entered in your KDP book setup.

If you use the free KDP ISBN, KDP fills this in for you. If you use your own, you enter it during setup and it appears on your cover template.
