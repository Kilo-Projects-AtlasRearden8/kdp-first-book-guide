# Categories

**Categories** tell Amazon where to shelve your book. They help readers find your book when browsing or searching. Picking good categories can make a big difference in whether people discover your book.

## How categories work on KDP

When you set up your book, you choose up to **three categories** (also called browse categories). Examples:

- Fiction > Mystery, Thriller & Suspense > Mystery > Cozy
- Nonfiction > Self-Help > Personal Growth
- Business & Money > Entrepreneurship

Amazon uses these to place your book in its store, and they affect which bestseller lists your book can appear on.

## How to choose categories

1. **Think like a reader.** Where would someone go to look for a book like yours?
2. **Look at similar books.** Find 3–5 books like yours on Amazon. Scroll to "Product Details" and note their categories.
3. **Be specific.** A narrower category (e.g., "Self-Help > Anxiety & Panic Disorders") is often easier to stand out in than a broad one (e.g., "Self-Help").
4. **Match your content.** Do not pick a category your book does not truly fit — it leads to unhappy readers and bad reviews.

## Tips for first-time authors

- Pick categories that actually describe your book, not just popular ones.
- You can request up to 10 total categories by contacting KDP support after your book is live (3 you choose at setup, plus up to 7 more via request).
- Categories can be changed later in KDP, so you are not locked in.

## Common fiction categories

- Mystery, Thriller & Suspense
- Romance
- Science Fiction & Fantasy
- Literature & Fiction
- Teen & Young Adult

## Common nonfiction categories

- Self-Help
- Health, Fitness & Dieting
- Business & Money
- Parenting & Relationships
- Education & Teaching

If your book is for healthcare workers (education only — never medical advice), you might look at Education & Teaching or Medical categories, but be careful not to imply your book gives medical advice.
