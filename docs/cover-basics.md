# Cover Basics

Your book cover is often the first thing a potential reader sees. It needs to look professional, communicate what your book is about, and stand out in a small thumbnail on Amazon.

For print books on KDP, the cover is a single **wrap-around** file that includes the front cover, the spine, and the back cover. For eBooks, you only need a front cover image.

## Front cover

The front cover should include:

- **Title:** Large and easy to read, even as a tiny thumbnail.
- **Subtitle (optional):** Helps explain what the book is about.
- **Author name:** Your name or pen name.
- **Imagery (optional):** A photo, illustration, or design that fits your genre.

**Tips:**
- Keep it simple. One strong image or idea is better than a cluttered design.
- Make sure the title is readable at thumbnail size (about 1 inch tall on screen).
- Avoid putting critical text too close to the edge.

## Spine (print only)

The spine shows on a bookshelf. It usually includes:

- Title (short version)
- Author name
- Publisher or imprint name (optional — you can use your own, like "Asdale Dynamics")

**Important:** The spine width depends on your page count and paper type. You cannot know the exact spine width until you know your final page count. Many authors finalize the interior first, then design the cover with the correct spine width.

## Back cover (print only)

The back cover usually includes:

- **Book description / blurb:** 2–4 sentences that make someone want to read the book.
- **Author bio:** A short paragraph about you, sometimes with a photo.
- **Barcode area:** KDP adds the barcode automatically. Leave space for it (lower right is standard).
- **ISBN:** Printed near the barcode (KDP handles this too).

## eBook cover

For Kindle eBooks, you only need the front cover image. It should be:

- A tall rectangle (for example, 2560 x 1600 pixels is a common good size)
- At least 1400 pixels on the longest side (Amazon recommends 2500+)
- JPEG or TIFF format
- RGB color
- Under 50 MB

## Design options

You have several options, from free to paid:

1. **Design it yourself** with a free tool like Canva. Use KDP's cover dimensions.
2. **Use KDP Cover Creator** — a free, simple tool inside KDP that builds a cover from your text and a background image. Good for beginners, limited design flexibility.
3. **Hire a designer** — best results, costs money. Look for someone who designs book covers specifically.

## KDP cover templates

KDP provides cover templates that show you exactly how big the front, spine, and back should be based on your trim size, page count, and paper type. Download the template after you set up your print book details, and use it in your design program.

### Using a template

1. Finish your manuscript and know your approximate page count.
2. In KDP, set up your paperback details (trim size, interior color, page count).
3. Download the cover template (a PDF with guidelines).
4. Open the template in your design tool (Canva, Photoshop, etc.) and design within the guidelines.
5. Export your cover as a print-ready PDF (for print) or JPEG (for eBook).

## File requirements

**Print cover:** A single PDF with front, spine, and back. Use KDP's template to get the right dimensions.

**eBook cover:** JPEG or TIFF, front cover only.
