# Trim Size

**Trim size** is the finished size of your print book — how wide and tall it is when printed and trimmed. Choosing a trim size is one of the first decisions you make, because it affects your page count, your cover dimensions, and your printing cost.

## Common KDP trim sizes

KDP offers many standard trim sizes. The most popular for beginners are:

| Trim size | Common uses | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| 5 x 8 inches | Fiction, memoirs, general nonfiction | Popular, portable, low printing cost |
| 5.5 x 8.5 inches | Fiction, nonfiction | Slightly larger, still common |
| 6 x 9 inches | Nonfiction, business, self-help | Very common for paperbacks |
| 6.14 x 9.21 inches | Nonfiction | A KDP standard size |
| 7 x 10 inches | Workbooks, guides, books with images | Larger, more pages fit |
| 8.25 x 8.25 inches | Children's books, photo books | Square, color printing |

There are many more sizes available. The full list is in your KDP account when you set up your paperback or hardcover.

## How to choose

Think about your book's purpose:

- **Novel or memoir:** 5 x 8 or 5.5 x 8.5 inches. Readers expect this size and it fits easily in a bag.
- **Nonfiction, self-help, business:** 6 x 9 inches. Looks substantial on a shelf.
- **Workbook, planner, or guide with worksheets:** 7 x 10 or 8.5 x 11 inches. More room to write.
- **Children's book or color photo book:** 8.5 x 8.5 or 8.5 x 11 inches. Color printing costs more, so keep page count in mind.

There is no wrong choice. Pick what feels right for your book. You can change it later before publishing, but changing it after your book is live means re-uploading the interior and cover.

## How trim size affects your manuscript

Your manuscript does not have a fixed page size until you set one. When you upload to KDP, you tell KDP the trim size, and KDP flows your text into pages of that size.

- A larger trim size fits more words per page, so your book may have fewer pages.
- A smaller trim size fits fewer words per page, so your book may have more pages.
- Page count affects your printing cost and the width of your book's spine.

If you have images or tables, check that they still look good at your chosen trim size.

## Setting trim size in KDP

When you create your paperback or hardcover in KDP, you choose the trim size from a dropdown. You do not need to set the page size in Word to match exactly — KDP handles the conversion — but it helps to format your manuscript with margins appropriate for the size you plan to use.

## Bleed

**Bleed** is when images or colors run all the way to the edge of the page, with no white margin. If your book has full-bleed images, you need to set up bleed in your manuscript and choose a trim size that supports it. Most text-only books do not need bleed.

- **No bleed:** Recommended for first books. Simpler and less likely to cause upload errors.
- **Bleed:** Required if your images go to the edge of the page. Adds complexity to formatting.
