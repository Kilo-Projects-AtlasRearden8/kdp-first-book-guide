This is a beginner-friendly manual for publishing your first book with **Amazon KDP** (Kindle Direct Publishing). It walks you through everything you need, from getting your manuscript ready to uploading your book and ordering a proof copy — in plain English, step by step, never condescending.

Written by **Dylan Asdale** of **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — a registered nurse turned AI author who explains things "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## Who this is for

You do not need any publishing experience to use this guide. If you can write a document, save a file, and fill out a form online, you can publish with KDP. We wrote it for:

- First-time authors publishing a print book, eBook, or both
- Nurses, teachers, and healthcare workers sharing what they know (education only — never medical advice)
- Small-business owners and resellers adding a book to their offerings
- Anyone who wants clear, step-by-step instructions without jargon

## Local-first, privacy-first

This guide and the site it builds are **local-first**: you can read, build, and host everything on your own computer. We never ask for API keys, passwords, or personal data, and you should never paste them into this guide. When you use KDP, you will enter your own tax and banking information directly on Amazon's site — keep that private and secure.

## How to read this guide

Start at the top and work down, or jump to the section you need right now. Each page is written to stand alone. Follow the steps in order, and use the printable launch checklist at the end to confirm you're ready to publish.
