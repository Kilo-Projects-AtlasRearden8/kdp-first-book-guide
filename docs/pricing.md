# Pricing & Royalties

Pricing is how much readers pay for your book. Royalties are how much you earn from each sale. This page explains both in plain English.

## How KDP pricing works

You set the **list price** — the price customers see on Amazon. When your book sells, Amazon keeps a portion and pays you the rest as a royalty. For print books, Amazon also subtracts the printing cost.

## eBook royalties (Kindle Direct Publishing)

You choose between two royalty options:

- **35% royalty:** Available in most countries. Fewer restrictions.
- **70% royalty:** Higher earnings, but with requirements:
  - List price between $2.99 and $9.99 (USD) in most marketplaces.
  - The book must be priced at least 20% below the physical book price, if a print edition exists.
  - Delivery costs are subtracted (based on file size).

Most first-time eBooks priced under $10 use the 70% option. If your eBook is large (lots of images), delivery costs can eat into the 70% royalty — check the KDP calculator.

## Paperback and hardcover royalties

For print books, your royalty is:

**List price − printing cost − Amazon's cut = your royalty**

Amazon's cut for print is typically **40%** of the list price for sales on Amazon, and **60%** for expanded distribution (bookstores, libraries). Printing cost depends on:

- Trim size
- Page count
- Black and white or color ink
- White or cream paper
- Marketplace (US, UK, EU, etc.)

### Example (illustrative only, not a guarantee)

A 300-page black-and-white paperback, 6 x 9 inches, US marketplace:

- List price: $12.99
- Estimated printing cost: ~$4.50
- Amazon's cut (40%): ~$5.20
- Your royalty: ~$3.29

Your actual numbers will differ. Always use KDP's royalty calculator in your account before setting a price.

## Setting your price

There is no perfect price. Consider:

- **Genre:** Fiction eBooks often sell at $2.99–$5.99. Nonfiction can support higher prices.
- **Page count:** Longer print books cost more to print, so they need a higher price to earn a reasonable royalty.
- **Your goals:** Launching low ($0.99–$2.99 eBook) can attract first readers. Higher prices earn more per sale but may sell fewer copies.
- **Competition:** Look at similar books in your category and price near them.

You can change your price at any time in KDP. Changing the price does not require a new review.

## Expanded distribution

KDP offers optional **expanded distribution**, which makes your print book available to bookstores, libraries, and online retailers beyond Amazon. It pays a lower royalty (because those sellers take a cut). It is optional — many first-time authors skip it and sell only on Amazon.

## Taxes and banking

To get paid, you must set up:

- **Tax information:** In the US, this is your tax interview (SSN or EIN). Asdale Dynamics, LLC would use its EIN. Non-US authors complete the appropriate tax form (often W-8BEN).
- **Bank information:** Where Amazon deposits your royalties.

Amazon pays royalties monthly, about 60 days after the end of the month in which the sale occurred, once you meet a minimum threshold (varies by country and payment method).
