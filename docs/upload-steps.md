# Upload Steps

This is the step-by-step process for uploading your book to Amazon KDP. Take it one step at a time. You do not have to finish in one sitting — KDP saves your work as a draft.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- Your manuscript file (.docx recommended)
- Your cover file (PDF for print, JPEG for eBook)
- Your book title and subtitle
- Your book description (blurb)
- Your categories and keywords
- Your pricing choice
- Your ISBN (or plan to use the free KDP ISBN)
- Your tax and bank information (for royalties)

## Step 1: Sign in to KDP

Go to [kdp.amazon.com](https://kdp.amazon.com) and sign in with your Amazon account. If you do not have an Amazon account, create one first.

**Security tip:** Use a strong, unique password and turn on two-factor authentication. You will be entering tax and banking information.

## Step 2: Create a new title

From your KDP dashboard, click **Create** → **Paperback** (or Hardcover, or Kindle eBook). This starts a new book project.

## Step 3: Enter book details

Fill in:

- Language
- Book title
- Subtitle (optional)
- Author name
- Contributors (optional)
- Description / blurb
- Publishing rights (you own the copyright, typically)
- Keywords (up to seven)
- Categories (up to three)
- Age range (optional)
- Publication date (you can choose today or a future date)

## Step 4: Choose ISBN

Decide:

- **Free KDP ISBN:** Amazon assigns one. Simple, free.
- **Your own ISBN:** Enter the ISBN you bought (e.g., from Bowker).

Select your choice and continue.

## Step 5: Set interior and paper options

For print books, choose:

- Interior type: Black & white or Color
- Paper color: White or Cream
- Paper type: White or Cream (varies by marketplace)
- Trim size: Choose from the list (see [Trim Size](trim-size.md))
- Bleed: Usually "No bleed" for a first book

## Step 6: Upload your manuscript

Upload your manuscript file (.docx). KDP will convert it and show you a preview. Check the preview carefully:

- Chapter headings look right
- Page breaks are in the right places
- No missing or garbled text
- Images look clear (if you have any)

If something looks wrong, fix your manuscript and re-upload.

## Step 7: Upload your cover

Upload your cover file. For print, this is the wrap-around PDF (front, spine, back). KDP will show a preview. Check:

- Title and author name are readable
- Spine is centered
- Back cover description looks right
- No important text is cut off

If you do not have a cover yet, you can use KDP Cover Creator to make a simple one.

## Step 8: Set pricing and royalty

Choose your marketplace(s) (e.g., Amazon.com). Set your list price. KDP shows your estimated royalty. Use the calculator to confirm the numbers work for you.

Choose royalty option for eBooks (35% or 70%).

## Step 9: Choose distribution

Decide whether to use **expanded distribution** (bookstores and libraries beyond Amazon). For a first book, many authors skip it.

For eBooks, decide whether to enroll in **KDP Select** (optional, makes your eBook exclusive to Amazon for 90 days in exchange for promotional tools). You can skip this.

## Step 10: Review and publish

Review every section one more time. When you are ready, click **Publish** (for print) or **Save and publish** (for eBook).

Your book goes into review. Amazon usually reviews within 24–72 hours, sometimes longer. You will get an email when it is live or if there are issues.

## After publishing

- Your book appears on Amazon.
- Order a **proof copy** (see [Proofing](proofing.md)) before telling the world, so you can see the physical book.
- Share the link with friends, family, and early readers.
- Ask readers to leave honest reviews.
