# Keywords

**Keywords** are words and phrases readers type into Amazon's search bar. Along with categories, they help shoppers find your book. KDP lets you enter up to **seven keywords** (or keyword phrases) when you set up your book.

## How keywords work

Amazon's search matches what shoppers type against your title, subtitle, description, and keywords. Good keywords connect your book with people looking for exactly what you wrote.

Keywords are not tags visible on your book's page. They are behind-the-scenes search terms.

## How to choose keywords

1. **Brainstorm.** What would you type to find a book like yours? Write down 20–30 phrases.
2. **Use Amazon's search bar.** Type a word or two and see what Amazon suggests. These are real searches shoppers make.
3. **Check similar books.** Look at books like yours and think about what words describe them.
4. **Be specific.** "Self-help" is too broad. "Self-help for new nurses" or "anxiety workbook for healthcare workers" targets real readers.
5. **Use phrases, not just single words.** "Intermittent fasting for beginners" works better than "fasting" or "beginners."

## Keyword rules

- You get up to seven keywords or keyword phrases.
- Each field can hold multiple words (up to 50 characters per field, roughly).
- Do not repeat your title or author name as a keyword — Amazon already knows those.
- Do not use other authors' names or brand names unless they are genuinely relevant.
- Do not use misleading keywords just to get traffic.

## Where to enter keywords

During KDP setup, in the book details section, there is a "Keywords" area with seven boxes. Enter one phrase per box. You can edit them later.

## Examples

If your book is a beginner's guide to using AI at work:

- AI for beginners
- artificial intelligence at work
- how to use AI tools
- AI for nurses
- AI productivity guide
- large language models explained
- free AI tools

If your book is a cozy mystery:

- cozy mystery
- amateur sleuth mystery
- small town mystery
- clean mystery
- mystery book club

## Update over time

If your book is not being found, try different keywords. You can change them in KDP at any time without republishing. Check your sales and search performance after a few weeks and adjust.
